# Learning and Development Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-009
**Last updated:** March 2025

## Purpose

Horizon Financial Services invests in the continuous professional development of its employees. This policy sets out the support available and the process for accessing it.

## Annual training budget

Each permanent employee has access to an **annual training budget of USD 2,500** per calendar year. The budget covers:

- External courses and workshops;
- Online learning subscriptions and platforms;
- Books and professional publications;
- Attendance at industry conferences (see below);
- Professional certifications and exam fees (see below).

The budget does **not** roll over into the following year. Unused budget is forfeited on 31 December.

## Approval process

All training expenditure must be pre-approved by the direct line manager. Approval is based on:

- The relevance of the training to the employee's current or expected future role;
- The likely business benefit to the team and firm;
- Availability of budget.

Requests should be submitted through the L&D portal at least 10 business days before the intended start of the training.

## Certifications

Professional certifications (for example, CFA, FRM, PMP, cloud and technology certifications) are supported as follows:

- **Exam fees are reimbursed 100% on first-attempt pass.**
- **Exam fees are reimbursed 50% on second-attempt pass.**
- No reimbursement is provided for failed attempts beyond the second, or where the employee does not sit the exam within 6 months of enrolment.
- Preparatory materials (books, courses) may be funded from the annual training budget subject to normal approval.

For certifications requiring multi-year commitment or programme fees exceeding USD 5,000 in total, a separate learning agreement is required. This may include a repayment clause if the employee leaves the firm within a specified period after completion.

## Conferences

Employees may attend up to **2 external conferences per calendar year**, subject to manager approval and available budget. Conference attendance requires:

- A brief written proposal setting out the expected benefit;
- A summary or knowledge-sharing session with the team on return.

Travel and accommodation for conferences are governed by the Business Travel Policy.

## Study leave

Employees preparing for approved professional exams may take up to **5 working days of paid study leave per calendar year**. Study leave must be pre-approved by the line manager and is separate from annual leave.

Study leave for exams not directly relevant to the employee's role is at the manager's discretion and may be granted as unpaid leave.

## Internal knowledge sharing

The firm actively encourages internal knowledge sharing. **Optional knowledge-sharing sessions are held on Friday afternoons between 4:00 PM and 5:00 PM**, hosted by different teams on a rotating basis. Employees are encouraged to attend and to volunteer to present.

## Mentoring

A voluntary mentoring programme is available to all permanent employees. Employees interested in being mentored, or in acting as a mentor, may register through the L&D portal.
