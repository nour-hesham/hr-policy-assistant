# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-003
**Last updated:** February 2025

## Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees whose role has been designated as eligible for hybrid working by the relevant department head. Not all roles are eligible; roles that require on-site presence (for example, certain operations, reception, and dealing-desk functions) are excluded.

## Eligibility

An employee is eligible to work remotely under this policy if:

- They have completed at least **6 months** of continuous service, and
- Their role has been designated as eligible by their department head, and
- Their line manager has approved a remote-working arrangement in writing.

## Standard arrangement

Eligible employees may work remotely for **up to 2 days per calendar week**. The specific days should be agreed in advance with the line manager and should provide adequate in-office overlap with the team.

Remote-working days are not an entitlement and may be adjusted or suspended by the line manager based on business needs, project demands, or performance considerations.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work remotely from outside their normal country of residence for a maximum of **15 consecutive calendar days** per year, subject to:

- Written notification to HR at least 10 working days in advance;
- Confirmation that the employee has appropriate connectivity and equipment;
- Confirmation that the arrangement does not create tax, legal or regulatory issues in the destination country.

Arrangements exceeding 15 days require additional review by HR and Tax, and may not be approved.

## Fully remote arrangements

Fully remote arrangements (no in-office days) are exceptional and are considered only for specific roles or specific personal circumstances (for example, medical needs). They require approval from the department head and HR.

## Equipment and expenses

The company provides:

- A standard company laptop;
- Access to required software and systems via VPN.

The company does **not** reimburse home internet, home office furniture, utilities or other domestic costs associated with remote working. Employees are responsible for ensuring their home working environment is safe and suitable.

## Availability and conduct

While working remotely, employees are expected to:

- Be available and responsive during core working hours as defined in the Work Hours policy;
- Attend all scheduled meetings via video where practical;
- Maintain the same standards of professionalism, confidentiality and data security as in the office;
- Ensure that client and internal information is not visible to, or overheard by, others in their remote location.

## Review and withdrawal

Remote-working arrangements are subject to ongoing review. The company reserves the right to withdraw or modify an arrangement at any time based on business need, performance, or breach of this policy, with reasonable notice where possible.
