# Business Travel Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-006
**Last updated:** April 2025

## Scope

This policy covers all business travel undertaken on behalf of Horizon Financial Services, whether domestic or international. Non-travel business expenses are governed by the separate Expense Claims and Reimbursement Policy.

## Approval

All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's direct line manager. International travel additionally requires approval from the department head.

Approval should be obtained **before** any bookings are made.

## Booking

All flights and hotels must be booked through the approved travel platform (**Horizon TravelHub**). Direct bookings outside the platform will not be reimbursed unless pre-authorised by finance for exceptional reasons.

## Air travel

- **Economy class** is the standard for all flights of less than 6 hours' scheduled flying time (per single leg).
- **Business class** is permitted for flights of **6 hours or more** scheduled flying time per single leg.
- **First class** is not reimbursed under any circumstances.
- Employees may voluntarily upgrade at their own expense.

## Accommodation

Hotel bookings should be selected from the approved travel platform, within the following nightly limits:

- **Domestic travel: up to USD 200 per night**
- **International travel: up to USD 280 per night**

Higher rates require pre-approval from the department head and may be permitted for specific cities where the standard limits are not practical.

## Ground transportation

- Taxis, rideshare and public transport are reimbursable against receipts.
- **Car rental requires pre-approval** from the line manager. Where approved, employees should select mid-size vehicles unless there is a business reason for a larger class.
- Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at the standard mileage rate published by finance.

## Per diem

A per diem is provided to cover meals and incidental expenses during business travel:

- **Domestic: USD 75 per full day**
- **International: USD 120 per full day**

The per diem is provided in lieu of individual meal receipts. Employees may not claim both per diem and meal receipts for the same day.

For partial travel days (fewer than 12 hours away from the primary work location), a half per diem applies.

## Trip reports

For any business trip of **more than 3 calendar days**, a brief trip report must be submitted to the line manager within **5 business days** of return. The report should summarise the business objectives, outcomes, and any follow-up actions.

## Personal travel combined with business trips

Employees may extend a business trip for personal reasons, subject to the following:

- The company will only cover the cost of the business portion (flights, accommodation and per diem for the business days only).
- Any additional cost of routing or timing changes for personal reasons is borne by the employee.
- Personal travel days must be recorded as annual leave.

## Travel insurance

Employees on approved business travel are covered by the corporate travel insurance policy. Details are available from HR. Personal travel extensions are not covered.
