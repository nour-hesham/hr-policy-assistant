# Expense Claims and Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-005
**Last updated:** April 2025

## Scope

This policy covers the reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees in the performance of their duties. It does not cover expenses relating to business travel, which are governed by the separate Business Travel Policy.

## General principles

- Expenses must be **reasonable, necessary and directly related to the performance of business duties**.
- Personal expenses will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.
- Employees are expected to select the most cost-effective option where practical.

## Receipts

- **Original itemised receipts are required for any expense of USD 25 or more.** Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.
- For expenses **below USD 25**, receipts are preferred but not mandatory. A brief description of the expense is required.
- Receipts must be in the employee's name or clearly linked to the business purpose.

## Submission and approval

- Claims must be submitted through the finance portal **within 30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred. Claims older than 30 days may be rejected.
- Each claim requires approval from the employee's direct line manager.
- Approved claims are processed by the finance team and reimbursed within **15 business days** of manager approval.

## Client entertainment

Expenses relating to client entertainment (meals, events, hospitality) are permitted subject to the following:

- Any client entertainment expense exceeding **USD 100 per occasion** requires pre-approval from the department head, in addition to line manager approval on submission.
- The name(s) of the client(s) hosted and the business purpose must be recorded on the claim.
- Alcohol may be included in client entertainment claims where appropriate, subject to local law and reasonable moderation.

## Company credit cards

Employees issued a company credit card must:

- Use the card only for approved business expenses;
- Reconcile card transactions through the finance portal within 15 business days of the statement date;
- Reimburse the company for any personal expenses inadvertently charged to the card within **5 business days** of identification.

Repeated or wilful misuse of a company credit card is a disciplinary matter and may result in withdrawal of the card and further action under the Code of Conduct.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are not reimbursable under this policy:

- Personal travel, meals or entertainment;
- Parking or traffic fines;
- Loss of personal property;
- Gifts to family or friends of employees;
- Any expense that has not been pre-approved where pre-approval is required.

## Currency

Expenses incurred in foreign currency should be claimed in USD, converted at the exchange rate published by the finance team on the date of the transaction, or the rate shown on the credit card statement.
