# Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** HR-POL-007
**Last updated:** January 2025

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour expected of all employees, contractors and representatives of Horizon Financial Services. It reflects the values on which the firm's reputation is built.

## Core principles

All employees are expected to act with **integrity, honesty and professionalism** in every aspect of their work. This includes:

- Treating colleagues, clients, counterparties and the public with respect and courtesy at all times;
- Avoiding any conduct that could reasonably be considered discriminatory, harassing, bullying or otherwise inappropriate;
- Complying with all applicable laws, regulations and internal policies.

## Confidentiality

Client information, firm proprietary information, and unpublished market information must be treated as strictly confidential. Confidential information may not be shared with anyone outside the firm, and may only be shared inside the firm on a strict need-to-know basis.

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose to their line manager and to Compliance any actual or potential conflict of interest, including:

- Personal financial interests in clients, counterparties, or competitors;
- Close personal relationships with colleagues, clients or counterparties that may affect professional judgement;
- Outside directorships or trusteeships.

Disclosure should be made promptly, in writing, and updated whenever circumstances change.

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept modest business gifts and hospitality subject to the following:

- Any gift or single item of hospitality with an estimated value **above USD 50** must be disclosed to Compliance.
- Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (including gift cards) must never be accepted, regardless of value.
- Gifts or hospitality that could reasonably be seen as intended to influence a business decision must be declined.

## Outside employment and activities

Any paid outside employment, consultancy or directorship must be disclosed to HR and requires written approval before it commences. Approval will consider potential conflicts of interest, demands on the employee's time, and reputational implications.

Unpaid activities (charitable, community, academic) do not generally require approval but should be disclosed if they may create a conflict of interest.

## Use of firm resources

Firm resources, including systems, equipment and information, are provided for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable, provided it does not interfere with work or breach any other policy.

## Reporting concerns

Employees who become aware of actual or suspected misconduct, whether by colleagues or by the firm, are encouraged to raise it. Concerns may be raised through any of the following channels:

- The employee's direct line manager;
- HR;
- Compliance;
- The **anonymous ethics hotline**, details of which are published on the internal portal.

The firm does not tolerate retaliation against any employee who raises a concern in good faith, whether the concern is ultimately substantiated or not.

## Breaches

Breaches of this Code may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and where appropriate, notification to regulators or law enforcement.
