# Sick Leave and Medical Absence Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-002
**Last updated:** March 2025

## Entitlement

Employees are entitled to up to **14 working days** of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not accrue and cannot be carried over into the following year.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and is not deducted from annual leave entitlement.

## Notification

Employees who are unable to attend work due to illness must notify their direct line manager as early as possible, and **no later than 9:00 AM on the day of absence**. Notification may be by phone, email or the HR portal.

Where an employee is unable to notify their manager personally (for example, due to hospitalisation), a family member or colleague may notify on their behalf.

## Medical certification

- For absences of **1 to 2 consecutive working days**, no medical certificate is required, though the employee may be asked to confirm the absence in writing on return.
- For absences of **3 or more consecutive working days**, a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner must be submitted to HR within 3 working days of return.
- Repeated short absences (more than 5 separate occurrences in a rolling 12-month period) may trigger a review with HR, regardless of duration.

## Extended medical leave

Absences of **more than 14 consecutive working days** are considered extended medical leave and require:

1. A medical certificate covering the full period of absence, and
2. An occupational health review before return to work.

Extended medical leave beyond the 14-day paid entitlement is unpaid, though the company operates a discretionary extended-illness scheme reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Employees should contact HR to discuss.

## Chronic and long-term conditions

Employees with chronic medical conditions or disabilities may agree a separate arrangement with HR, in which case related absences are not counted against the standard sick-leave entitlement. Reasonable accommodations will be considered in line with applicable law.

## Return to work

Employees returning from any absence of 5 or more consecutive working days will have a brief return-to-work conversation with their line manager. The purpose is to confirm fitness to work and to identify any support the employee may need.

## Misuse

Sick leave is intended solely for genuine illness or injury. Suspected misuse (for example, taking sick leave to extend a holiday) will be investigated and may lead to disciplinary action.
