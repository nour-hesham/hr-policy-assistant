# Annual Leave Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-001
**Last updated:** March 2025

## Purpose

This policy sets out how annual leave is accrued, requested, approved and carried over at Horizon Financial Services.

## Entitlement

- Standard entitlement is **21 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Employees who have completed **5 or more years** of continuous service are entitled to **25 working days**.
- Employees who have completed **10 or more years** of continuous service are entitled to **28 working days**.

Public holidays are additional and do not count against annual leave entitlement.

## Accrual

Annual leave accrues on a monthly basis at a rate of one-twelfth of the annual entitlement per completed calendar month of service. New joiners accrue from their start date on a pro-rata basis.

## Requesting leave

Requests must be submitted through the HR portal.

- For any leave request of **more than three consecutive working days**, a minimum of **two weeks' notice** is required.
- For shorter requests, notice of at least three working days is preferred.

Requests are subject to approval by the employee's direct line manager, based on business needs and adequate coverage. Where a leave request is declined, the manager must provide a reason in writing and offer alternative dates within the same quarter where possible.

If a leave dispute cannot be resolved with the direct manager, the matter may be escalated to the relevant department head.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over up to **5 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar year. Any carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the following year, after which they are forfeited.

Carryover beyond 5 days is only permitted in exceptional circumstances (for example, extended business-critical projects) and requires written approval from HR in addition to the line manager.

## Leave during notice periods

Employees serving a notice period following resignation or termination may take annual leave during the notice period only with explicit written approval from their line manager. The company reserves the right to require the employee to take, or not to take, accrued leave during the notice period.

## Payment in lieu

Payment in lieu of untaken annual leave is only made on termination of employment, and is calculated on a pro-rata basis for the current calendar year.
