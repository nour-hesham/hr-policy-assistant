# Performance Review Process

**Document ID:** HR-POL-010
**Last updated:** February 2025

## Purpose

Horizon Financial Services operates a structured performance-review cycle to ensure that all employees receive regular, meaningful feedback on their performance and development.

## Review cycle

The performance year runs from **1 January to 31 December**.

There are two formal touch-points during the year:

- **Annual review** conducted in Q1 of the following year (typically January to March), covering the previous full performance year.
- **Mid-year check-in** conducted in Q3 (typically July to September), providing an interim assessment and an opportunity to adjust goals for the second half of the year.

Managers are also expected to hold regular one-to-one conversations with each direct report at least monthly. These are less formal but are an important part of ongoing feedback.

## 360-degree feedback

The annual review incorporates **360-degree feedback**. Feedback is solicited from:

- The employee's direct line manager (mandatory);
- The employee themselves via a self-assessment (mandatory);
- Two to four peers (nominated by the employee and the manager);
- Any direct reports (mandatory for people managers).

Peer and report feedback is aggregated and anonymised before being shared with the employee.

## Rating scale

Employees receive an overall rating on the following four-point scale:

- **Exceeds Expectations** — consistently delivers beyond the expectations of the role.
- **Meets Expectations** — consistently delivers what the role requires; the expected rating for a fully performing employee.
- **Partially Meets Expectations** — some aspects of performance are below expectations and require improvement.
- **Does Not Meet Expectations** — performance is materially below expectations across multiple areas.

In addition, employees receive qualitative feedback on strengths and development areas.

## Development plans

- All employees, regardless of rating, receive a personal development plan covering the coming year.
- Employees rated **Partially Meets** or **Does Not Meet** are placed on a **formal performance improvement plan (PIP)** with specific, time-bound objectives. PIPs are typically 3 to 6 months in duration and are reviewed with the line manager and HR.
- Failure to achieve the objectives of a PIP may result in further action under the disciplinary or capability procedures.

## Promotions

Promotion decisions are made annually as part of the performance review cycle. To be considered for promotion, an employee normally needs to have:

- Been in their current role for at least 12 months;
- Received a rating of Meets Expectations or higher in the most recent annual review;
- Demonstrated readiness for the responsibilities of the next level.

## Compensation

Compensation reviews are conducted **separately** from the performance review process, though the outcome of the performance review is an important input. The details of the compensation review process, salary bands and bonus scheme are set out in separate documentation available from HR and are not covered by this policy.

## Confidentiality

Individual performance ratings and feedback are confidential. They are shared only with the employee, their line manager, HR, and other parties on a legitimate need-to-know basis (for example, in the context of a promotion decision).
